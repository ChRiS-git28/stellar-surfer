## ✦ 1.0.7 — Solar Flare

- **Star Shards follow your class**: Nebula slows and reveals, Sun ignites, Neptune weighs down, Aurora heals you, Betelgeuse bursts.
- Sun fire is now a **Solar Flare**: burns like fire but ignores invulnerability frames, with solar flames.
- *Cometary Recoil* becomes **Comet Stride**: move freely while firing.
- Black Hole and White Hole move **smoothly** when steered.
- **Flash finale** when a constellation completes; thinner links.
- Thorns damage no longer drags you down; Enhanced AI zombies no longer bite back after cosmic hits.
- Legendary Survival Overhaul / Tough As Nails: no more thirst.

Requires **Stellar Surfer — Cosmic Visuals 1.0.7**. Found a bug? https://github.com/ChRiS-git28/stellar-surfer/issues
