## ✦ 1.1.0 — First full release

Everything from the betas since 1.0.3, plus:
- **Meteor Rain trial rebuilt**: closed arena (you cannot leave the ring), real 3D meteors with fire trails, red warning rings, escalating rain.
- **Solar Flare**: 2 damage per second, flames clearly visible without shaders.
- **Constellations**: slightly smaller, links drawn cleanly from star to star (no more floating half-links).

Highlights of the betas: five classes with passives, auras and class Star Shards; Solar Flare; Event Horizon / Light Curtain vs projectiles and spells (Iron's Spells, Hazen n Stuff, Ars); Starlight Veil, dodge parry, no friendly fire; temperature / thirst mod compatibility; NeoForge jar.

Requires **Stellar Surfer — Cosmic Visuals 1.1.0**. Found a bug? https://github.com/ChRiS-git28/stellar-surfer/issues
