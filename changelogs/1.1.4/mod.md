## ✦ 1.1.4

All-in-one NeoForge version of Stellar Surfer (datapack + Cosmic Visuals resource pack in a single jar). Do not install the datapack or resource pack zips alongside it.

- Custom particles are now visible without shaders: every effect switched from additive to translucent blending, and the wisp, twinkle and aurora veil textures are more opaque.

Requires **Origins** and **Apoli** (NeoForge), Minecraft 1.21.1. Found a bug? https://github.com/ChRiS-git28/stellar-surfer/issues
