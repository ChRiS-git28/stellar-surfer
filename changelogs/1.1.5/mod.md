## ✦ 1.1.5

The NeoForge version now has its own client visual layer (gameplay is unchanged and identical to the datapack).

- New constellation skill screen: a turning five-pointed star at the heart, one colour and star model per class, rays, orbiting motes, flowing light, unlock animations, pan and zoom.
- Glow halos on orbs, motes and constellation stars, visible without shaders.
- Stellar Surfer skin for the Origins window, only while Stellar Surfer is shown (other origins keep your theme).
- Round orb particles in end-rod style; symmetric five-branch skill tree.

Requires **Origins** and **Apoli** (NeoForge), Minecraft 1.21.1, on client and server. Do not install the datapack or resource pack zips alongside this jar. Found a bug? https://github.com/InLighTs28/stellar-surfer/issues
