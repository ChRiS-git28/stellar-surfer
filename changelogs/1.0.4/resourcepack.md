## ✦ 1.0.4 — Little Star

### Changed
- Astrolabe: the 10 Rank stars are big, clear crosses when lit and small dim points when not.
- Cosmic effects now use **cutout** rendering: water and glass stay visible behind them (sharper edges; orb shells and the beam haze stay soft).
- Texts for the Starlight Veil, starglow and smaller body.

### Also in this update (1.0.3 — Old Ruins)
- Constellation animation fixed.
- Breath cooldown indicator, annotated Celestial Sigil diagram, new Graviton badge icons.

Requires **Stellar Surfer 1.0.4** (datapack).
