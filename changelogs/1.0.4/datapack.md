## ✦ 1.0.4 — Little Star

### Body & survival
- The Surfer is now **25% smaller** (hitbox and collisions follow).
- **Starlight Veil**: projectile damage -40% before Rank 3, -25% until Rank 7, then -15%.
- **Dodging burns nearby projectiles** (tridents and enchanted weapons just drop).
- Low Grace no longer gives Darkness: a soft **starglow** follows you underground and in the Nether.
- **No friendly fire** on villagers, traders, golems, allays, animals and tamed pets (MineColonies, MCA Reborn and Guard Villagers too).

### Movement & powers
- Climb and dive much faster while surfing.
- Magnetised items **spiral around you** several times before reaching you, with a trail of sparkles.
- `/function stlboy:debug/rank_up` gives +1 Rank instantly (testing).

### Also in this update (1.0.3 — Old Ruins)
- Wormhole, Rank 10: step into its heart to go **home with your allies**, once per Minecraft day.
- New skills **Event Horizon** (Rank 7) and **Light Curtain** (Rank 4): your Wormhole and your beam destroy projectiles.
- Trial shrines are now overgrown ruins with a column of light. Graviton gathers Gravity Sense and Stellar Magnetism, with new icons.
- Breath cooldown on the HUD, lighter HUD, constellation animation fixed, `/function stlboy:debug/trials`.

Requires **Stellar Surfer — Cosmic Visuals 1.0.4**. Found a bug? https://github.com/ChRiS-git28/stellar-surfer/issues
