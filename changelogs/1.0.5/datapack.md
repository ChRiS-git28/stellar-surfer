## ✦ 1.0.5 — Drifting Light

### Fixes
- Black Hole and White Hole follow your aim smoothly again.

### Changes
- Constellations now lie **flat (horizontal)** around the target.
- Stellar Magnetism: items **glide slowly** to you with custom sparkles. With several Surfers, the highest Rank wins; at equal Rank, the closest.
- **Temperature immunity** with Legendary Survival Overhaul, Tough As Nails and Cold Sweat.
- Every vanilla particle of the origin is now a **custom particle**.
